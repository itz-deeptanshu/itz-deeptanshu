## About Me

I'm an AI/ML engineer-in-the-making who likes taking a model from a messy dataset all the way to a working API. My days look like this:

- Exploring data (EDA) until the patterns show up, then building ML and deep learning models around them
- Training and iterating on models in **PyTorch**
- Serving them through fast, clean **FastAPI** backends
- Grinding problems on LeetCode and chasing that jump from TLE to Accepted
- Building in public and shipping at hackathons
